# 📊 Data Science Salaries Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing data science salaries, experience levels, employment types, company sizes, geographical distribution, and job-title-wise compensation.

## 📊 Dashboard Preview

![Data Science Salaries Dashboard](Ds_Salaries.png)

*Dashboard preview featuring salary analysis by experience level, company-size distribution, country-wise salary mapping, employee residence rankings, employment type, and job-title-wise salary comparisons.*

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores the `ds_salaries` dataset using Microsoft Power BI to analyze salary patterns and employment characteristics across the data science industry.

The dashboard combines KPI-style comparisons, bar and line charts, doughnut charts, a geographical map, and a detailed salary table. These visualizations help users explore compensation across experience levels, compare employment categories, examine company sizes, and investigate salary differences by job title and country.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze salary trends across experience levels.
- Compare salary-related measures across employment types.
- Examine the distribution of companies by size and location.
- Visualize average salary by country.
- Identify the most frequently represented employee residences.
- Compare average salaries across job titles and experience levels.
- Present salary data through clear and interactive visualizations.

## 🔍 Dashboard Features

### 💰 1. Salary Analysis by Experience Level

- Compares experience categories such as Senior, Intermediate, Entry Level, and Executive.
- Combines a column chart with a line chart.
- Displays employment-record counts alongside a salary-related measure.

### 🏢 2. Company Size and Location

- Visualizes the distribution of companies by size.
- Includes Small, Medium, and Large categories.
- Displays counts and proportions for each category.

### 🌍 3. Average Salary by Country

- Uses a geographical map to visualize salary information across countries.
- Supports geographical exploration of salary patterns.
- Helps compare the locations represented in the dataset.

### 👥 4. Top 10 Employee Residences

- Ranks employee residence locations by record count.
- Highlights the United States (US), Great Britain (GB), Canada (CA), Germany (DE), and other represented locations.
- Provides a quick comparison of the most frequently represented residences.

### 📈 5. Experience-Level Distribution

- Visualizes the distribution of records across experience categories.
- Includes Entry Level, Intermediate, Senior, and other available categories.
- Helps users understand the composition of the dataset.

### 💼 6. Employment Type Distribution

- Displays the distribution of employment types.
- Includes Full-time and other employment categories represented in the data.
- Helps explore the employment structure of the dataset.

### 📋 7. Average Salary by Job Title and Experience Level

The dashboard includes a detailed table with:

- Job title
- Experience level
- Average salary

This view supports comparisons between professional roles and experience categories, including AI Scientist, Analytics Engineer, Applied Data Scientist, and other roles.
